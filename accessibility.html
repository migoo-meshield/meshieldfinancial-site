Accessibility Statement

Last updated: August 4, 2026

ME Shield Financial Services is committed to making its website and services accessible to people with disabilities. We aim to improve keyboard navigation, readable contrast, text alternatives, clear labels, and compatibility with common assistive technologies.

Need assistance?

If you encounter an accessibility barrier or need information in another format, contact us at (407) 267-2652 or info@meshieldfinancial.com. Please identify the page and the difficulty encountered. We will make reasonable efforts to provide the information or service through an accessible alternative.

Ongoing improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We welcome feedback and periodically review the website as content and technology change.
