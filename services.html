Infinite Banking Concept (IBC)

Konsèp Bank Enfini (IBC)

Most families keep their money in banks and credit unions, letting someone else control the flow. IBC flips that: you use a specially-designed whole life insurance policy as your own private banking system — building cash value you can access on your terms, while staying protected for life.

Pifò fanmi kenbe lajan yo nan bank ak koperativ kredi, kite yon lòt moun kontwole fli a. IBC ranvèse sa a: ou itilize yon polis asirans lavi antye ki fèt espesyalman kòm pwòp sistèm bank prive ou — bati valè kach ou ka jwenn dapre kondisyon pa ou, pandan w rete pwoteje pou lavi.

This isn't a quick scheme — it's a long-term strategy that rewards patience and discipline. We'll walk you through exactly how it works before you decide if it's right for you.

Sa a se pa yon konplo rapid — se yon estrateji alontèm ki rekonpanse pasyans ak disiplin. Nou pral pase ou atravè egzakteman kijan li fonksyone anvan ou deside si li bon pou ou.
