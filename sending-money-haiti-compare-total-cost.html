Sending Money to Family in Haiti: Compare the Total Cost

Voye Lajan Bay Fanmi Ayiti: Konpare Pri Total la

Look beyond the advertised transfer fee and compare what your family will actually receive.

Pa gade sèlman frè yo fè reklam lan: konpare sa fanmi ou ap resevwa toutbon.

Begin with the family need

Kòmanse ak bezwen fanmi an

Helping family is easier to sustain when the transfer has a place in your household budget. Decide the amount and purpose before choosing the provider. Ask whether the recipient needs bank deposit, mobile delivery or cash pickup. An affordable transfer that cannot be collected conveniently may not solve the problem.

Ede fanmi pi fasil pou kenbe lè transfè a gen plas li nan bidjè kay ou. Deside montan ak objektif la anvan ou chwazi konpayi an. Mande si moun nan bezwen depo labank, livrezon mobil oswa retrè kach. Yon transfè bon mache moun nan pa ka resevwa fasil ka pa rezoud pwoblèm nan.

Compare the same transaction

Konpare menm tranzaksyon an

For a fair comparison, use the same amount, destination currency, payment method and delivery method at about the same time. Look at the total debited, fees, exchange rate, expected delivery and amount received. A zero-fee promotion can still deliver less because of the exchange rate. Do not assume an introductory offer is the regular price.

Pou konpare jis, itilize menm montan, lajan destinasyon, metòd peman ak livrezon nan apeprè menm moman. Gade total yo pran sou kont ou, frè, to chanj, delè prevwa ak montan resevwa. Yon pwomosyon san frè ka toujou bay mwens akoz to chanj lan. Pa sipoze yon òf premye transfè se pri nòmal la.

A simple hypothetical comparison

Yon senp konparezon ipotetik

Suppose you have a total budget of $105. Provider A charges $5 and converts $100 at an illustrative 130 units per dollar: 13,000 units. Provider B charges $0 and converts $105 at 122: 12,810 units. A delivers 190 more units in this invented example. These are not current Haitian exchange rates or provider quotes. Compare the actual disclosures before each payment.

Sipoze bidjè total ou se $105. Konpayi A pran $5 epi li chanje $100 nan yon to ipotetik 130 inite pou yon dola: 13,000 inite. Konpayi B pran $0 epi li chanje $105 nan 122: 12,810 inite. A bay 190 inite anplis nan egzanp envante sa a. Sa yo pa to chanj Ayiti aktyèl ni quote konpayi. Konpare vrè enfòmasyon yo anvan chak peman.

Confirm details through a trusted channel

Konfime detay nan yon kanal ou fè konfyans

Check the recipient name and delivery information before sending. If someone suddenly requests a new account or urgent payment, call a number you already know. Keep the receipt and transaction reference securely. Do not publish identification documents, receipts or account numbers in a community group to ask for help.

Verifye non benefisyè ak detay livrezon anvan ou voye. Si yon moun toudenkou mande yon nouvo kont oswa peman ijan, rele yon nimewo ou te deja konnen. Kenbe resi ak referans tranzaksyon an an sekirite. Pa pibliye pyès idantite, resi oswa nimewo kont nan gwoup kominote pou mande èd.

If the transfer goes wrong

Si transfè a gen pwoblèm

Contact the provider immediately and keep a record of the problem and response. Cancellation and error-resolution rights depend on the transfer and applicable rules; consult the CFPB’s current consumer guidance. ME Shield shares general education and is not acting as a money-transfer provider or recommending a specific company.

Kontakte konpayi an touswit epi kenbe dosye pwoblèm nan ak repons li. Dwa anilasyon ak koreksyon erè depann de transfè a ak règ ki aplike; konsilte gid konsomatè CFPB aktyèl la. ME Shield pataje edikasyon jeneral, li pa aji kòm konpayi transfè lajan ni li pa rekòmande yon konpayi espesifik.
