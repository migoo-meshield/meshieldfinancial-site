How to Choose a Financial Advisor You Can Actually Trust

Kijan pou Chwazi yon Konseye Finansye Ou Ka Reyèlman Fè Konfyans

Miguelson Etienne
Founder, ME Shield Financial Services
Fondatè, ME Shield Financial Services

Anyone can put "financial advisor" in a job title. Far fewer actually earn the trust that title implies. If you've ever left a meeting with an advisor feeling more confused than when you walked in, you already know the difference between someone who sells you a product and someone who's actually looking out for you.

Nenpòt moun ka mete "konseye finansye" nan yon tit travay. Pi piti ladan yo reyèlman touche konfyans tit sa a vle di. Si ou te janm kite yon reyinyon ak yon konseye santi ou plis konfonn pase lè ou te antre, ou deja konnen diferans ant yon moun ki vann ou yon pwodui ak yon moun ki reyèlman gade pou ou.

Look for Someone Who Explains Before They Sell

Chèche Yon Moun Ki Eksplike Anvan Yo Vann

A trustworthy advisor wants you to understand what you're buying and why — not just sign where indicated. If every answer to your questions leads quickly back to "just trust me" or rushes past the details, that's worth noticing.

Yon konseye ou ka fè konfyans vle ou konprann sa ou achte ak poukisa — pa jis siyen kote yo endike. Si chak repons a kesyon ou rapidman tounen nan "jis fè m konfyans" oswa kouri pase detay yo, sa vo lapèn remake.

Ask How They're Compensated

Mande Kijan Yo Konpanse

It's a completely fair question to ask any financial professional: how do you get paid on this? Commission-based, fee-based, or a mix — none of these are automatically bad, but you deserve to know which incentives are in the room with you.

Se yon kesyon konplètman jis pou mande nenpòt pwofesyonèl finansye: kijan ou touche sou sa a? Baze sou komisyon, baze sou frè, oswa yon melanj — okenn ladan yo pa otomatikman move, men ou merite konnen ki ankourajman ki nan chanm la avèk ou.

Notice Whether They Ask About You First

Remake Si Yo Mande Sou Ou Anvan

A good first meeting spends more time on questions about your situation than on pitching products. If a recommendation shows up before your actual goals, income, and concerns have even been discussed, that's a sign the advice is coming from a script rather than a conversation.

Yon bon premye reyinyon pase plis tan sou kesyon sou sitiyasyon ou pase sou prezante pwodui. Si yon rekòmandasyon parèt anvan objektif aktyèl ou, revni, ak enkyetid ou menm te diskite, sa se yon siyal konsèy la soti nan yon script olye yon konvèsasyon.

Continuity Matters More Than People Realize

Kontinwite Enpòtan Plis Pase Moun Reyalize

When you call with a question six months from now, will you reach the same person who set up your plan — or whoever happens to be available that day? Large call-center-style operations often can't offer this. Smaller, locally-rooted practices usually can, and that continuity directly affects how well your advisor actually knows your situation over time.

Lè ou rele ak yon kesyon sis mwa apati kounye a, èske ou pral rive jwenn menm moun ki te mete plan ou sou pye — oswa nenpòt moun ki disponib jou sa a? Gwo operasyon style sant apèl souvan pa ka ofri sa. Pratik ki pi piti, ki gen rasin lokal, abityèlman ka, epi kontinwite sa a afekte dirèkteman kijan konseye ou reyèlman konnen sitiyasyon ou avèk tan.

Trust Your Comfort Level With Communication

Fè Konfyans Nan Nivo Konfò Ou Ak Kominikasyon

If understanding your own finances matters to you, working with someone in a language you're not fully comfortable in adds a real barrier — even if everyone's trying their best. There's no reason to settle for half-understood advice about something this important.

Si konprann pwòp finans ou enpòtan pou ou, travay ak yon moun nan yon lang ou pa konplètman alèz ladan ajoute yon vrè baryè — menm si tout moun ap fè pi byen yo. Pa gen rezon pou aksepte konsèy mwatye-konprann sou yon bagay ki enpòtan konsa.

  • Good signs: Questions about your situation come before recommendations. Fees and incentives are explained clearly when asked. You leave understanding why, not just what.
  • Bon siyal: Kesyon sou sitiyasyon ou vini anvan rekòmandasyon. Frè ak ankourajman eksplike klèman lè yo mande. Ou kite konprann poukisa, pa jis ki sa.
  • Worth questioning: Pressure to decide quickly. Vague answers about how they're compensated. A different person handling your account every time you call.
  • Vo lapèn kesyone: Presyon pou deside rapidman. Repons vag sou kijan yo konpanse. Yon moun diferan k ap jere kont ou chak fwa ou rele.
This article offers general guidance on evaluating financial professionals and is not a recommendation of any specific advisor or firm. Always do your own research before making financial decisions.Atik sa a bay gid jeneral sou evalye pwofesyonèl finansye epi se pa yon rekòmandasyon nenpòt konseye oswa konpayi espesifik. Toujou fè pwòp rechèch ou anvan ou pran desizyon finansye.

The right advisor for you is the one who makes you feel more in control of your finances after every conversation, not less. That feeling is worth paying attention to.

Bon konseye pou ou se moun ki fè ou santi ou gen plis kontwòl sou finans ou apre chak konvèsasyon, pa mwens. Santiman sa a vo lapèn prete atansyon.
