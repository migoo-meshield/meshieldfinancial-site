## Voice

- First person, one real person talking ("I", "me"), never a corporate "we" that hides who is behind it. Plain, warm, direct, a little community-rooted.
- Short sentences. Say the specific thing (the form number, the document, the state), not a slogan.
- Explain first, never pressure. "You decide once you understand."
- It must not read as AI-written. Avoid: "navigating", "journey", "peace of mind", "tailored", "seamless", "unlock", "empower", "whether you're...", "in today's fast-paced world", "game-changer", stacked "not X, but Y" lines, and lots of em dashes. Prefer commas and periods.
- Haitian Creole: write natural Kreyòl, not word-for-word English. Keep numbers, form names and the website as they are. Have Migoo approve any new Kreyòl call to action.

| IBC Certified | IBC Practitioner |

The required disclaimers may still say "not an attorney" and "not legal advice". That is a disclaimer, not a service description.

## Required on every piece

- **Social posts and captions:** end with "Want to learn more? Connect with me here: connect.meshieldfinancial.com" (or a close variant Migoo approved). Never name a platform ("follow us on Instagram"); hashtags are fine.
- **Anything about Infinite Banking:** include "Educational content only, not financial advice." Never imply guaranteed growth, tax-free income or a quick fix. Policy loans accrue interest and reduce the death benefit if not repaid.
- **Immigration:** say "forms filing" or "document preparation". Never promise an outcome or a processing time.
- **Tax:** never promise a refund amount, "every deduction" or "maximize your refund".
- **Insurance:** never say "best price" or "best coverage". Say we compare carriers and show the options. Mention that quotes depend on the person and carrier.
- **Emails:** plain first-person text, sent from info@meshieldfinancial.com, with the unsubscribe link and the standard disclaimer footer.
- **Blog posts:** substantial, sourced where they cite figures, with a clear call to action. Pace: two or more a week.

## Never

- Invent facts, numbers, testimonials, reviews or client stories. Only use real Google reviews with the reviewer's words unchanged.
- Ask anyone to send an SSN, passport number, bank account or routing number in a message, post or comment.
- Say or imply that a tax return will show ME Shield as the preparer firm.
- Use "Trusted by 10,000+", "loved by", star counts or similar claims without a source Migoo confirms.
- Post, schedule or send anything. Drafts go to Migoo first.

## Output format for drafts

1. Three options with genuinely different angles (for example personal story, educational list, direct).
2. A 30 to 45 second video script: Hook, Body, CTA (the CTA points to connect.meshieldfinancial.com, never a platform).
3. Then a short check against this file: words, CTA, disclaimers, claims.
