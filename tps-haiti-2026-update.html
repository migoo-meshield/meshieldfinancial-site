TPS for Haiti in 2026: What the Latest USCIS Guidance Actually Means for You

TPS Pou Ayiti nan 2026: Sa Dènye Direktiv USCIS La Vle Di Pou Ou
← Back to Blog ← Retounen nan Blòg
ImmigrationImigrasyon·July 2026
This is a fast-moving legal situation. Guidance has changed multiple times in 2026. Always confirm your individual case status directly with USCIS or a licensed immigration attorney before making decisions.

On July 1, 2026, USCIS issued new guidance regarding Temporary Protected Status (TPS) for Haiti, which supersedes an earlier termination notice from March 25, 2026. If you or a family member holds TPS based on Haiti's designation, here's what's known so far — and what remains uncertain.

What's Changed

The new USCIS guidance reflects ongoing litigation affecting how TPS terminations are being processed. In practical terms, this means the situation for Haiti TPS holders has shifted again since earlier in the year, and previous termination notices may no longer be the final word for everyone affected.

Work Permits (EADs) — A Key Detail

Some TPS-linked Employment Authorization Documents with category codes A12 or C19 currently have a temporary "placeholder" extension of work authorization through July 10, 2026. This is described as limited relief tied to ongoing court orders, not a permanent resolution — meaning this date could change again depending on how the courts rule.

Why This Matters for Our Community

Because TPS for Haiti affects so many families in the Haitian diaspora, we know this uncertainty is stressful. Employers are being advised to closely monitor USCIS updates and be prepared to reverify affected employees' work authorization when required — which means it's worth checking with your employer's HR department as well as USCIS directly.

We are not attorneys. ME Shield Financial Services provides immigration forms filing assistance only — we help you complete and submit forms correctly. We do not provide legal advice on complex or contested immigration status questions like TPS eligibility disputes. For anything involving your specific TPS case or a court-related question, please consult a licensed immigration attorney.

What You Can Do Right Now

  • Check the official USCIS News & Alerts page directly for the most current information
  • Keep copies of your current TPS notices, EAD, and any prior USCIS correspondence in one place
  • If your EAD category is A12 or C19, mark July 10, 2026 on your calendar and check for updates before that date
  • If you need help filing related paperwork, we can walk you through the forms — step by step, in English or Kreyòl

Nan 1 Jiyè 2026, USCIS pibliye nouvo direktiv konsènan Estati Pwoteje Tanporè (TPS) pou Ayiti, ki ranplase yon avi terminezon pi bonè ki soti 25 Mas 2026. Si ou oswa yon manm fanmi ou gen TPS baze sou designasyon Ayiti, men sa nou konnen jiska prezan — ak sa ki toujou ensèten.

Sa Ki Chanje

Nouvo direktiv USCIS la reflete pwosè tribinal ki ap kontinye ki afekte kijan terminezon TPS yo trete. An tèm pratik, sa vle di sitiyasyon pou moun ki gen TPS Ayiti chanje ankò depi pi bonè nan ane a, epi avi terminezon anvan yo ka pa dènye pawòl la pou tout moun afekte.

Pèmi Travay (EAD) — Yon Detay Kle

Kèk Dokiman Otorizasyon Travay ki konekte ak TPS ki gen kòd kategori A12 oswa C19 kounye a gen yon ekstansyon tanporè "plasman" otorizasyon travay jiska 10 Jiyè 2026. Sa dekri kòm soulajman limite ki lye ak lòd tribinal ki ap kontinye, se pa yon rezolisyon pèmanan — sa vle di dat sa a ka chanje ankò depann sou kijan tribinal yo deside.
