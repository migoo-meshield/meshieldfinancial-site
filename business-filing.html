COMMON QUESTIONS
KESYON KOMEN

Business Filing Questions

Kesyon Sou Depo Biznis

Can you tell me which business structure is best for me?

Èske ou ka di m ki estriktire biznis ki pi bon pou mwen?

We’re not attorneys or CPAs, so we don’t advise on which legal structure fits your situation best. We handle the document filing once you’ve decided, and can refer you to an attorney or CPA for that decision if needed.

Nou pa avoka oswa CPA, kidonk nou pa konseye sou ki estriktire legal ki pi bon pou sitiyasyon ou. Nou jere depo dokiman an yon fwa ou deside, epi nou ka refere ou a yon avoka oswa CPA pou desizyon sa a si nesesè.

How long does LLC formation take?

Konbyen tan fòmasyon LLC pran?

Florida processing times vary, but filings are often processed within a matter of days once submitted correctly.

Tan tretman Florid varye, men depo yo souvan trete nan yon ti tan kèk jou yon fwa yo soumèt kòrèkteman.

What happens if I miss my Annual Report deadline?

Sa k ap pase si mwen rate dat limit Rapò Anyèl mwen?

Florida can administratively dissolve your LLC for missing it. We help make sure that never happens by tracking and filing it for you.

Florid ka dissoud LLC ou administrativman pou rate li. Nou ede asire sa pa janm rive lè nou swiv ak depoze li pou ou.
