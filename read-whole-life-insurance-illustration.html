Before Infinite Banking: How to Read a Whole Life Illustration

An illustration is a comparison tool

Yon ilistrasyon se yon zouti konparezon

Before discussing Infinite Banking, understand the insurance contract that would support the strategy. An illustration shows policy values under stated assumptions. It is not a bank statement or a promise that the non-guaranteed column will happen. IBC is a use of cash-value life insurance, not a separate government-approved account or a deposit product.

Anvan ou pale Bank Enfini, konprann kontra asirans ki ta sipòte estrateji a. Yon ilistrasyon montre valè polis la anba sipozisyon yo bay. Se pa yon relevé labank ni yon pwomès kolòn ki pa garanti a ap rive vre. IBC se yon fason itilize asirans lavi ki gen valè kach, li pa yon kont apa gouvènman an apwouve ni yon depo labank.

Read premiums and surrender values together

Li prim ak valè racha ansanm

Find the planned premium, how long it is payable and whether riders or extra payments are optional. Then find the cash surrender value available if you exit, not just the death benefit. Example: $6,000 of illustrative first-year premiums and $1,800 of surrender value do not mean $6,000 is available for immediate borrowing. Early access can be limited and leaving early can be costly.

Jwenn prim yo prevwa a, pandan konbyen tan ou dwe peye li ak si rider oswa peman anplis yo opsyonèl. Apre sa jwenn valè racha ki disponib si ou sòti, pa sèlman benefis lanmò. Egzanp: $6,000 prim premye ane ak $1,800 valè racha nan yon egzanp pa vle di ou ka prete $6,000 touswit. Aksè bonè ka limite epi kite polis la bonè ka koute chè.

Separate guaranteed and non-guaranteed columns

Separe kolòn garanti ak sa ki pa garanti

Ask the agent to point out every guaranteed value and the assumptions behind other values. Participating-policy dividends are not guaranteed. Example: a projection using current dividends can look stronger than the guaranteed scenario; compare both before judging affordability. Do not treat a projected result as a savings-account interest rate or compare it with an investment return without accounting for insurance costs.

Mande ajan an montre chak valè garanti ak sipozisyon lòt valè yo. Dividann polis patisipatif pa garanti. Egzanp: yon pwojeksyon ki itilize dividann aktyèl yo ka pi bèl pase senaryo garanti a; konpare toulede anvan ou jije si ou ka kenbe prim yo. Pa trete yon pwojeksyon kòm to enterè kont epay ni konpare l ak rannman envestisman san konte pri asirans.

Stress-test the plan

Teste plan an nan kondisyon difisil

Ask for scenarios with lower dividends, a delayed extra contribution and the borrowing pattern you actually expect. Would the premium still fit after a job change? What happens if you need cash in year two rather than year ten? Request the loan rate, rider charges, surrender conditions and tax-sensitive limits in writing. A hypothetical worksheet is a planning aid, not a personalized recommendation.

Mande senaryo ak dividann pi ba, yon kontribisyon anplis ki anreta ak fason ou prevwa prete toutbon. Èske prim lan toujou antre nan bidjè a si ou chanje travay? Kisa k ap pase si ou bezwen kach nan dezyèm ane olye dizyèm ane? Mande to prè, frè rider, kondisyon racha ak limit ki gen konsekans taks alekri. Yon tablo ipotetik ede planifye, li pa yon rekòmandasyon pèsonalize.

Decide whether the long commitment fits

Gade si angajman alontèm nan adapte

Review immediate cash needs, emergency savings, debt and the actual need for life insurance before committing. Whole life is not the right tool for every household. Keep the signed illustration and ask for periodic in-force reviews after purchase. ME Shield explains insurance options; a tax professional should evaluate tax consequences for your circumstances.

Revize bezwen kach rapid, epay ijans, dèt ak vrè bezwen asirans lavi anvan ou angaje. Lavi antye pa bon zouti pou chak fanmi. Kenbe ilistrasyon siyen an epi mande revizyon polis an vigè regilyèman apre acha. ME Shield esplike opsyon asirans; yon pwofesyonèl taks dwe evalye konsekans taks selon sitiyasyon ou.
