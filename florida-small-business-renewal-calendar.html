A Florida Small-Business Renewal Calendar You Can Actually Maintain

Separate Sunbiz filings, local receipts, insurance renewals and tax obligations in one practical calendar.

One business can have several clocks

Yon biznis ka gen plizyè kalandriye

An LLC filing does not replace every other business obligation. Create a calendar with the responsible agency, requirement, due date, preparation date, person responsible and proof of completion. Start from your actual registration and renewal notices rather than copying another company’s schedule. Requirements vary with location, activity and tax classification.

Depoze LLC pa ranplase tout lòt obligasyon biznis. Kreye yon kalandriye ak ajans responsab, egzijans, dat limit, dat preparasyon, moun responsab ak prèv travay la fini. Kòmanse ak vrè anrejistreman ak avi renouvèlman ou yo olye ou kopye kalandriye yon lòt konpayi. Egzijans yo varye selon kote, aktivite ak klasifikasyon taks.

Keep Sunbiz current

Kenbe Sunbiz ajou

Review the official annual-report instructions and your entity record. Check the registered agent, mailing address, managers or authorized persons and contact email. The report updates state records; it is not an income tax return. Confirm the applicable filing year, deadline and fees directly with Sunbiz. A formation year and the first annual-report year may differ.

Li enstriksyon ofisyèl rapò anyèl la ak dosye antite ou a. Verifye ajan anrejistre, adrès lapòs, manadjè oswa moun otorize ak imel kontak. Rapò a mete dosye eta a ajou; se pa yon deklarasyon taks sou revni. Konfime ane, dat limit ak frè ki aplike yo dirèkteman sou Sunbiz. Ane fòmasyon an ak premye ane rapò anyèl la ka diferan.

Add the obligations outside Sunbiz

Ajoute obligasyon ki pa sou Sunbiz

List city and county business tax receipts if applicable, professional licenses, insurance and any DBA renewal. Separately list federal and state tax filings that apply to your business. Example: renewing an LLC record does not renew an insurance license or tell your city about an address change. Verify each issuing authority separately; an update at one agency may not update another.

Mete resi taks biznis vil ak konte si sa aplike, lisans pwofesyonèl, asirans ak renouvèlman DBA. Fè yon lis apa pou depo taks federal ak eta ki aplike. Egzanp: renouvle dosye LLC pa renouvle lisans asirans ni li pa avèti vil la ou chanje adrès. Verifye chak otorite apa; mete yon ajans ajou pa toujou mete lòt yo ajou.

Use a preparation date before the deadline

Mete yon dat preparasyon anvan dat limit la

Suggested workflow: review each renewal 30 days ahead, collect documents and confirm cost, then verify completion before the due date. These are organizational reminders, not legal deadlines. Example: a policy expiring December 1 gets a November 1 review and a separate confirmation task. Store the renewal receipt and the updated certificate, not just a screenshot of a payment page.

Woutin sijere: revize chak renouvèlman 30 jou davans, rasanble dokiman ak konfime pri, epi verifye li fini anvan dat limit la. Sa yo se rapèl òganizasyon, se pa dat limit legal. Egzanp: yon polis ki ekspire 1 desanm jwenn yon revizyon 1 novanm ak yon travay apa pou konfimasyon. Sove resi renouvèlman ak nouvo sètifika a, pa sèlman foto paj peman an.

Make the calendar useful every month

Fè kalandriye a itil chak mwa

Choose one monthly review day. Mark an item complete only after checking the official record or receiving confirmation. Give a backup person a safe way to find the calendar, without sharing passwords in plain text. ME Shield can assist with filing organization; legal structure and tax treatment questions need appropriately qualified professionals.

Chwazi yon jou revizyon chak mwa. Make yon travay fini sèlman apre ou verifye dosye ofisyèl la oswa resevwa konfimasyon. Bay yon moun ranplasman yon fason sekirize pou jwenn kalandriye a san pataje modpas an tèks klè. ME Shield ka ede òganize depo yo; kestyon estrikti legal ak tretman taks mande pwofesyonèl ki kalifye pou sa.
