What You Need Before Starting Your Immigration Paperwork

Sa Ou Bezwen Anvan Ou Kòmanse Papye Imigrasyon Ou
Miguelson Etienne
Founder, ME Shield Financial Services
Fondatè, ME Shield Financial Services

One of the most common things I hear from clients is: "I didn't know I needed all of this." Immigration paperwork can feel overwhelming, not because any single form is impossible, but because getting organized before you start makes the entire process smoother — and reduces the chance of delays caused by missing documents.

Youn nan bagay mwen tande pi souvan nan bouch kliyan yo se: "Mwen pa t konnen mwen te bezwen tout sa a." Papye imigrasyon ka santi twòp, pa paske nenpòt fòm sèl enposib, men paske òganize anvan ou kòmanse fè tout pwosesis la pi fasil — epi redui chans reta ki koze pa dokiman ki manke.

Personal Identification Documents

Dokiman Idantifikasyon Pèsonèl

  • Valid passport (and any prior passports, if applicable)
  • Paspò valab (ak nenpòt ansyen paspò, si aplikab)
  • Birth certificate — with certified translation if it's not in English
  • Sètifika nesans — ak tradiksyon sètifye si li pa an angle
  • Marriage certificate, if applicable
  • Sètifika maryaj, si aplikab
  • Divorce decree, if applicable
  • Dekrè divòs, si aplikab
  • Any prior immigration documents you've received (approval notices, receipt notices, prior visas)
  • Nenpòt dokiman imigrasyon ou te resevwa deja (avi apwobasyon, avi resi, ansyen viza)

Proof of Relationship (If Applicable)

Prèv Relasyon (Si Aplikab)

For family-based filings, you'll typically need documentation proving your relationship to the petitioner or beneficiary: marriage certificates and evidence of a shared life together (joint bank accounts, leases, photos), birth certificates showing parent-child relationships, and any prior name changes with supporting documentation.

Pou depo ki baze sou fanmi, ou pral tipikman bezwen dokimantasyon ki pwouve relasyon ou ak petisyonè a oswa benefisyè a: sètifika maryaj ak prèv yon lavi pataje ansanm (kont labank konjwen, kontra lokasyon, foto), sètifika nesans ki montre relasyon paran-pitit, ak nenpòt chanjman non ak dokimantasyon sipò.

Financial Documentation

Dokimantasyon Finansye

Many immigration forms require proof that a sponsor can financially support the applicant: recent tax returns (typically the last 3 years), pay stubs or proof of income, and an employment verification letter.

Anpil fòm imigrasyon mande prèv ke yon esponnsò ka sipòte aplikan an finansyèman: deklarasyon taks resan (tipikman dènye 3 ane yo), souch peman oswa prèv revni, ak yon lèt verifikasyon travay.

Photographs

Foto

Some forms require passport-style photos that meet specific size and background requirements. Check the current instructions for your exact form before submission.

Kèk fòm mande foto stil paspò ki satisfè egzijans espesifik pou gwosè ak fon. Verifye enstriksyon aktyèl pou fòm egzak ou anvan ou soumèt li.

A Note on What I Do (and Don't Do)

Yon Nòt sou Sa M Fè (ak Sa M Pa Fè)

I want to be upfront about this: my role is Immigration Forms Filing Assistance — helping you accurately prepare and file the correct forms with the required supporting documentation. For complex cases involving denials, appeals, or removal proceedings, you'll want to work with an appropriate licensed professional. What I can do is make sure your paperwork is organized, complete, and filed correctly the first time, which prevents the delays that come from incomplete submissions.

Mwen vle klè sou sa a: wòl mwen se Asistans Depo Fòm Imigrasyon — ede ou prepare presizeman ak depoze bon fòm yo ak dokimantasyon sipò ki nesesè. Pou ka konplèks ki enplike refi, apèl, oswa pwosedi ranvwa, ou ta vle travay ak yon pwofesyonèl lisansye apwopriye. Sa m ka fè se asire papye ou òganize, konplè, epi depoze kòrèkteman premye fwa a, sa ki anpeche reta ki soti nan soumisyon enkonplè.

Why Getting Organized Early Matters

Poukisa Òganize Bonè Enpòtan

Missing required evidence can lead USCIS to request additional information and may delay a case. Organizing the documents listed in the current form instructions before you begin can reduce avoidable omissions.

Prèv obligatwa ki manke ka fè USCIS mande plis enfòmasyon epi sa ka retade yon dosye. Òganize dokiman ki nan enstriksyon aktyèl fòm nan anvan ou kòmanse ka diminye omisyon ki ka evite.

Document requirements vary by form and individual situation. Review the instructions for your specific form on USCIS.gov, then use our immigration form preparation service page if you want help organizing and preparing your filing package.

Dokiman yo mande yo varye selon fòm nan ak sitiyasyon chak moun. Verifye enstriksyon fòm espesifik ou sou USCIS.gov, epi gade paj sèvis preparasyon fòm imigrasyon nou an si ou bezwen èd pou òganize ak prepare dosye ou.
