Tax Preparation Questions

Kesyon Preparasyon Taks

At a minimum, bring: W-2 or 1099 forms, your Social Security Number (or ITIN), your prior-year tax return (if available), and your bank account info for direct deposit. If you're self-employed, also bring records of income and business expenses. We'll guide you through everything in your consultation.

Omwen, pote: fòm W-2 oswa 1099, Nimewo Sekirite Sosyal ou (oswa ITIN), deklarasyon taks ane anvan ou (si disponib), ak enfòmasyon kont bank ou pou depozit dirèk. Si ou travay pou tèt ou, pote tou dosye revni ak depans biznis. Nou pral gide ou nan tout bagay nan konsiltasyon ou.

Yes. We assist clients with ITIN (Individual Taxpayer Identification Number) applications. An ITIN allows you to file taxes, open a bank account, and build financial history — even without a Social Security Number.

Wi. Nou ede kliyan ak aplikasyon ITIN (Nimewo Idantifikasyon Kontribyab Endividyèl). Yon ITIN pèmèt ou deklare taks, louvri yon kont bank, epi bati istwa finansyè — menm san Nimewo Sekirite Sosyal.

Yes. We can prepare and file prior-year returns and amendments. If you missed a year or filed incorrectly, it's better to correct it proactively. The IRS generally allows up to 3 years to claim a refund.

Wi. Nou ka prepare epi deklare deklarasyon ak amandman ane anvan. Si ou rate yon ane oswa ou te deklare mal, li pi bon pou korije sa proaktivman. IRS a jeneralman pèmèt jiska 3 an pou reklame yon ranbousman.

Yes. We handle tax returns for sole proprietors, LLCs, and small businesses including Schedule C filings. If you're a small business owner, we can also help identify deductions you may be missing.

Sa depan sou konpleksite fòm nan ak vitès ou bay dokiman sipò obligatwa yo. Fòm senp ka pran 3–5 jou ouvrab; pakè ki pi konplèks ka pran 1–2 semèn. Nou pral ba ou yon kalandriye klè nan konsiltasyon ou.

Business Filing

Depo Biznis

Yes — we prepare and file Articles of Organization with the Florida Division of Corporations, along with related paperwork like EIN applications and registered agent designation. For deciding which business structure is best for your situation, we recommend consulting a CPA or business advisor first.

Wi — nou prepare ak depoze Atik Òganizasyon ak Divizyon Konpayi Florid, ansanm ak papye ki gen rapò tankou aplikasyon EIN ak designasyon ajan anrejistre. Pou deside ki estriktire biznis ki pi bon pou sitiyasyon ou, nou rekòmande konsilte yon CPA oswa yon konseye biznis anvan.

Document filing means accurately completing and submitting the forms your business needs — Articles of Organization, EIN applications, DBA registration, annual reports. Professional advice means recommending a specific business structure, drafting custom contracts, or resolving disputes — and that requires a business lawyer. We handle the paperwork and connect you with the right professional when needed.

Depo dokiman vle di konplete ak soumèt avèk presizyon fòm biznis ou bezwen yo — Atik Òganizasyon, aplikasyon EIN, enskripsyon DBA, rapò anyèl. Konsèy legal vle di rekòmande yon estriktire biznis espesifik, ekri kontra pèsonalize, oswa rezoud konfli — epi sa mande yon avoka lisansye. Nou jere premye a epi nou refere dezyèm la.

Yes — Florida LLCs and corporations must file an Annual Report each year to stay active. We can prepare and submit it for you, and remind you before your deadline so your business doesn't get administratively dissolved.

Wi — LLC ak konpayi Florid yo dwe depoze yon Rapò Anyèl chak ane pou rete aktif. Nou ka prepare ak soumèt li pou ou, epi fè ou sonje anvan dat limit ou pou biznis ou pa dissoud administrativman.

Infinite Banking Questions

Kesyon sou Bank Enfini

IBC is a strategy where you use a specially-designed, dividend-paying whole life insurance policy as your own private banking system. You build cash value, borrow against it on your own terms, and repay yourself — keeping your money working for you while you use it. It's a long-term wealth-building strategy, not a separate insurance product.

IBC se yon estrateji kote ou itilize yon polis asirans lavi antye ki fèt espesyalman, ki peye dividann, kòm pwòp sistèm bank prive ou. Ou bati valè kach, prete kont li dapre pwòp kondisyon ou, epi ou ranbouse tèt ou — kenbe lajan ou ap travay pou ou pandan ou itilize li. Se yon estrateji bati richès alontèm, se pa yon pwodui asirans separe.

A savings account pays low interest and your money sits idle while the bank lends it out to others. With IBC, your cash value grows inside the policy and you can borrow against it whenever you want — the policy keeps growing as if you never withdrew anything, and you control the repayment terms instead of a bank.

Yon kont epay peye ba enterè epi lajan ou rete san fè anyen pandan bank lan prete li bay lòt moun. Ak IBC, valè kach ou grandi anndan polis la epi ou ka prete kont li nenpòt lè ou vle — polis la kontinye grandi tankou ou pa janm retire anyen, epi ou kontwole kondisyon ranbousman an olye de yon bank.

Not every whole life policy is built for IBC. It needs to be specially structured — with the right riders and dividend design — so cash value grows quickly and access to it stays flexible. We design the policy specifically for this purpose rather than using an off-the-shelf plan.

Se pa chak polis lavi antye ki bati pou IBC. Li bezwen estriktire espesyalman — ak bon avantaj ak konsepsyon dividann — pou valè kach la grandi rapid epi aksè a li rete fleksib. Nou fèt polis la espesyalman pou objektif sa a olye de itilize yon plan estanda.

IBC is a long-term strategy, not a quick win. Meaningful cash value typically builds over the first few years, and the real advantages compound the longer the policy runs. It rewards patience and consistent funding — we're upfront about this before anyone starts.

IBC se yon estrateji alontèm, se pa yon viktwa rapid. Valè kach enpòtan tipikman bati sou premye kèk ane yo, epi vrè avantaj yo miltipliye plis polis la kontinye. Li rekonpanse pasyans ak finansman konsistan — nou klè sou sa a anvan nenpòt moun kòmanse.

No — while business owners and high savers often benefit most, IBC can work for anyone who can commit to consistent funding over time. What matters more than income level is your ability to stay disciplined with contributions. We'll help you figure out honestly whether it fits your situation.

Non — pandan ke mèt biznis ak moun ki ekonomize anpil souvan jwenn plis benefis, IBC ka fonksyone pou nenpòt moun ki ka angaje nan finansman konsistan avèk tan. Sa ki pi enpòtan pase nivo revni se kapasite ou pou rete disipline ak kontribisyon yo. Nou pral ede ou dekouvri onètman si li adapte ak sitiyasyon ou.

No credit check or approval process — the cash value is already yours. As long as there's enough built up, you can access it, and you set your own repayment schedule. The insurance company simply uses your policy's cash value as collateral.

Pa gen verifikasyon kredi ni pwosesis apwobasyon — valè kach la se pou ou deja. Depi gen ase bati, ou ka aksede li, epi ou fikse pwòp orè ranbousman ou. Konpayi asirans lan senpleman itilize valè kach polis ou a kòm garanti.
