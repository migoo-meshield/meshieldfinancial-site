USCIS Documents and Translations: Organize the File Before You Submit

Dokiman USCIS ak Tradiksyon: Òganize Dosye a Anvan Ou Soumèt Li

How to organize identity records, complete translations and a final document check without confusing document assistance with legal advice.

Start with instructions for your exact form

Kòmanse ak enstriksyon fòm egzak ou a

A document checklist from a friend is a starting point for questions, not proof of what your case requires. Use the current USCIS page for the exact form and read its evidence instructions. Make columns for required item, person, original language, translation and completion status. Legal eligibility, filing strategy and unusual evidence questions belong with an immigration attorney or authorized accredited representative.

Yon lis yon zanmi ba ou se yon pwen depa pou kestyon, li pa prèv sa dosye pa ou mande. Itilize paj USCIS aktyèl la pou fòm egzak la epi li enstriksyon prèv yo. Fè kolòn pou dokiman obligatwa, non moun, lang orijinal, tradiksyon ak estati preparasyon. Kestyon kalifikasyon legal, estrateji depo ak prèv espesyal dwe ale jwenn yon avoka imigrasyon oswa reprezantan akredite otorize.

Keep the original-language document with its translation

Kenbe dokiman lang orijinal la ak tradiksyon li

Foreign-language evidence generally needs a full English translation and the translator’s certification of accuracy, completeness and competence. Check the particular instructions for additional requirements. A short summary is not the same as translating the full document. Include seals, annotations and reverse-side information where present; ask the translator to address unclear text rather than inventing it.

Prèv nan lòt lang anjeneral bezwen yon tradiksyon Anglè konplè ak sètifikasyon tradiktè a sou presizyon, entegralite ak konpetans li. Verifye enstriksyon espesifik yo pou lòt egzijans. Yon rezime pa menm ak tradiksyon dokiman an antye. Mete so, nòt ak enfòmasyon sou do dokiman an kote yo prezan; mande tradiktè a trete tèks ki pa klè olye li envante l.

Check names and dates carefully

Verifye non ak dat avèk anpil atansyon

Example: a birth record shows 04/07, but the translation writes July 4 without checking the source date format. That small mismatch can create confusion. Compare names, spelling, dates and parents’ information across the original and translation. Do not silently change a name to make documents match. Ask a qualified legal professional how to address genuine discrepancies.

Egzanp: yon ak nesans montre 04/07 men tradiksyon an ekri 4 jiyè san verifye fòma dat orijinal la. Ti diferans sa a ka kreye konfizyon. Konpare non, òtograf, dat ak enfòmasyon paran yo ant orijinal la ak tradiksyon an. Pa chanje yon non an silans pou fè papye yo matche. Mande yon pwofesyonèl legal kalifye kijan pou trete vrè diferans yo.

Build a file someone else can follow

Bati yon dosye yon lòt moun ka konprann

Use clear filenames such as applicant-birth-record-original and applicant-birth-record-translation. Check scans for cut-off edges, unreadable stamps and missing pages. Example: a two-sided document scanned only on the front is not complete just because the PDF opens. Keep a final copy of exactly what was submitted and the submission confirmation in a secure location.

Itilize non fichye klè tankou aplikan-ak-nesans-orijinal ak aplikan-ak-nesans-tradiksyon. Verifye si scan yo koupe rebò, si so yo lizib ak si pa manke paj. Egzanp: yon dokiman ki gen de fas pa konplè si ou scan sèlman devan, menm si PDF la ouvri. Kenbe yon kopi final egzak sa ou te soumèt ak konfimasyon an nan yon kote sekirize.

Know the boundary of document assistance

Konnen limit asistans dokiman

ME Shield’s role is document preparation assistance, not selecting your immigration benefit, representing you or guaranteeing approval. Ask a lawyer or authorized representative about eligibility, status, deadlines, prior denials or removal proceedings. Fees, form editions and filing methods can change; recheck the official form page immediately before submitting.

Wòl ME Shield se asistans preparasyon dokiman, li pa chwazi benefis imigrasyon ou, reprezante ou oswa garanti apwobasyon. Pale ak yon avoka oswa reprezantan otorize sou kalifikasyon, estati, dat limit, refi anvan oswa pwosedi depòtasyon. Frè, edisyon fòm ak fason depo ka chanje; verifye paj ofisyèl la ankò jis anvan ou soumèt.
