Umbrella and Flood Insurance: The Coverage Most Floridians Overlook

Asirans Umbrella ak Inondasyon: Kouvèti Pifò Floridyen Neglije
Miguelson Etienne
Founder, ME Shield Financial Services
Fondatè, ME Shield Financial Services

Two of the most overlooked types of coverage also happen to be two of the most important if the worst actually happens: umbrella insurance and flood insurance. Neither is included automatically in a standard home or auto policy.

De nan kalite kouvèti ki pi neglije yo rive tou se de nan pi enpòtan yo si pi mal la reyèlman rive: asirans umbrella ak asirans inondasyon. Ni youn ni lòt pa enkli otomatikman nan yon kontra kay oswa machin estanda.

What Is Umbrella Insurance?

Kisa Asirans Umbrella Ye?

An umbrella policy adds extra liability coverage on top of your existing home and auto policies — typically starting at $1 million. If you're ever sued for more than your home or auto policy's liability limit covers (a serious car accident, for example), umbrella coverage is what protects your savings, your home equity, and your future income from being at risk. It's also one of the more affordable types of coverage relative to the protection it provides.

Yon kontra umbrella ajoute kouvèti responsablite siplemantè anwo kontra kay ak machin ou deja genyen — tipikman kòmanse nan $1 milyon. Si yo janm pousuiv ou pou plis pase limit responsablite kontra kay oswa machin ou kouvri (yon aksidan machin grav, pa egzanp), kouvèti umbrella se sa ki pwoteje ekonomi ou, kapital kay ou, ak revni fiti ou pou yo pa nan risk. Se tou youn nan kalite kouvèti ki pi abòdab konpare ak pwoteksyon li bay.

Why Flood Insurance Is a Separate Policy

Poukisa Asirans Inondasyon se yon Kontra Separe

This surprises a lot of people: standard homeowners insurance does not cover flood damage, period — even if the flooding is caused by a hurricane. Flood insurance is its own policy, available through the National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP) or private carriers. And you don't have to live in a high-risk flood zone to need it — a significant share of flood claims each year come from homes outside the officially mapped high-risk zones.

Sa a sipriz anpil moun: asirans kay estanda pa kouvri domaj inondasyon, pwen final — menm si inondasyon an te lakòz pa yon siklòn. Asirans inondasyon se pwòp kontra pa li, ki disponib atravè National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP) oswa konpayi prive. Epi ou pa oblije viv nan yon zòn risk elve pou bezwen li — yon pòsyon enpòtan nan reklamasyon inondasyon chak ane soti nan kay ki andeyò zòn risk elve ki make ofisyèlman yo.

Who Actually Needs These?

Kiyès ki Reyèlman Bezwen Sa yo?

Umbrella coverage makes sense for almost any homeowner, and especially anyone with significant savings, home equity, a teen driver in the household, or a pool (a common source of liability claims). Flood insurance makes sense for any Florida homeowner or renter, regardless of flood zone, given how much of the state sits at or near sea level.

Kouvèti umbrella gen sans pou prèske nenpòt pwopriyetè kay, e espesyalman nenpòt moun ki gen ekonomi enpòtan, kapital kay, yon jèn chofè nan fwaye a, oswa yon pisin (yon sous komen pou reklamasyon responsablite). Asirans inondasyon gen sans pou nenpòt pwopriyetè kay oswa lokatè Florid, kèlkeswa zòn inondasyon, konsidere konbyen nan eta a chita nan oswa tou pre nivo lanmè.

What I'd Recommend Doing This Week

Sa Mwen Ta Rekòmande Fè Semèn Sa a

  1. Ask what your current home and auto liability limits actually are, and whether an umbrella policy would close the gap.
  2. Mande ki sa limit responsablite kay ak machin aktyèl ou yo reyèlman ye, ak si yon kontra umbrella ta fèmen twou a.
  3. Check your flood zone designation — and remember that "not high-risk" doesn't mean "no risk."
  4. Tcheke deziyasyon zòn inondasyon ou — epi sonje "pa gen risk elve" pa vle di "pa gen risk."
  5. If you don't have flood insurance, get a quote before hurricane season, since new flood policies typically have a 30-day waiting period before they take effect.
  6. Si ou pa gen asirans inondasyon, jwenn yon estimasyon anvan sezon siklòn, paske nouvo kontra inondasyon tipikman gen yon peryòd datant 30 jou anvan yo aktive.
