Term Life vs. Whole Life vs. IBC: Which Is Right for Your Family?

Lavi Tèm vs Lavi Antye vs IBC: Ki Sa Ki Bon Pou Fanmi Ou?

Miguelson Etienne
Founder, ME Shield Financial Services
Fondatè, ME Shield Financial Services

Choosing life insurance isn't really about finding the cheapest policy — it's about deciding what you need that policy to do for your family over the next 10, 20, or 30 years. Term, whole life, and Infinite Banking (IBC) all use the same basic tool — a life insurance death benefit — but they're built for very different goals.

Chwazi asirans lavi se pa reyèlman jwenn polis ki pi bon mache — se desizyon sa ou bezwen polis sa a fè pou fanmi ou pandan 10, 20, oswa 30 ane k ap vini. Tèm, lavi antye, ak Bank Enfini (IBC) tout itilize menm zouti debaz la — yon benefis lanmò asirans lavi — men yo bati pou objektif trè diferan.

Term Life Insurance

Asirans Lavi Tèm

Term life covers you for a fixed period — typically 10, 20, or 30 years — and pays a death benefit only if you pass away during that term. It has no cash value and expires worthless if you outlive it.

Asirans lavi tèm kouvri ou pou yon peryòd fiks — tipikman 10, 20, oswa 30 ane — epi peye yon benefis lanmò sèlman si ou mouri pandan tèm sa a. Li pa gen valè kach epi li ekspire san valè si ou viv pi lontan pase li.

Best for: Covering a specific need with a clear end date — like the years your kids are dependent, or the length of a mortgage. Term gives you the most death benefit for the lowest premium, which is why it's often recommended as a baseline protection.

Pi bon pou: Kouvri yon bezwen espesifik ak yon dat fen klè — tankou ane pitit ou yo depandan, oswa longè yon ipotèk. Tèm ba ou plis benefis lanmò pou pi ba prim, se poutèt sa yo souvan rekòmande l kòm pwoteksyon debaz.

Whole Life Insurance

Asirans Lavi Antye

Whole life covers you for your entire life as long as premiums are paid, and a portion of every premium builds guaranteed cash value over time. Premiums are higher than term, but they never increase, and the policy never expires while it's in force.

Lavi antye kouvri ou pou tout vi ou depi prim yo peye, epi yon pati nan chak prim bati valè kach garanti avèk tan. Prim yo pi wo pase tèm, men yo pa janm ogmante, epi polis la pa janm ekspire pandan li an fòs.

Best for: Permanent needs — final expenses, estate planning, leaving a guaranteed legacy — and for people who want a conservative savings component growing alongside their protection.

Pi bon pou: Bezwen pèmanan — depans final, planifikasyon eritaj, kite yon eritaj garanti — ak pou moun ki vle yon eleman ekonomi konsèvatif k ap grandi akote pwoteksyon yo.

Infinite Banking (IBC)

Bank Enfini (IBC)

IBC isn't a separate product — it's a whole life policy specifically designed and funded to maximize early cash value, so you can actively borrow against it the way you'd use a bank, while the death benefit continues to protect your family.

IBC se pa yon pwodui separe — se yon polis lavi antye espesyalman fèt ak finanse pou maksimize valè kach bonè, pou ou ka aktivman prete kont li menm jan ou ta itilize yon bank, pandan benefis lanmò a kontinye pwoteje fanmi ou.

Best for: Families who want permanent protection and a self-directed source of liquidity for opportunities — real estate, business investment, paying off debt on their own terms — and who can commit to the policy long enough for it to build meaningful cash value.

Pi bon pou: Fanmi ki vle pwoteksyon pèmanan ak yon sous likidite ki dirije pa tèt yo pou opòtinite — imobilye, envestisman biznis, peye dèt sou pwòp tèm yo — epi ki ka angaje nan polis la lontan ase pou li bati valè kach enpòtan.

Side-by-Side Comparison

Konparezon Kote Pou Kote

  • Premium cost: Term is lowest, whole life and IBC are higher because they're permanent and build cash value.
  • Pri prim: Tèm se pi ba, lavi antye ak IBC pi wo paske yo pèmanan epi yo bati valè kach.
  • Duration: Term ends; whole life and IBC last your entire life as long as funded.
  • Dire: Tèm fini; lavi antye ak IBC dire tout vi ou depi yo finanse.
  • Cash value: Term has none; whole life builds it steadily; IBC is structured to build it faster and make it more accessible.
  • Valè kach: Tèm pa gen okenn; lavi antye bati l tikras tikras; IBC estriktire pou bati l pi vit epi fè l plis aksesib.
  • Flexibility while alive: Term offers none; whole life offers some access to cash value; IBC is specifically optimized for active use of that cash value.
  • Fleksibilite pandan ou vivan: Tèm pa ofri okenn; lavi antye ofri yon ti aksè a valè kach; IBC espesyalman optimize pou itilizasyon aktif valè kach sa a.
This comparison is meant to help you understand your options, not to replace personalized advice. The right fit depends on things unique to you — your age, health, goals, and budget — so a conversation with a licensed insurance professional is a great next step.Konparezon sa a fèt pou ede ou konprann opsyon ou yo, se pa pou ranplase konsèy pèsonalize. Bon chwa a depann sou bagay ki inik pou ou — laj ou, sante ou, objektif ou, ak bidjè ou — kidonk yon konvèsasyon ak yon pwofesyonèl asirans lisansye se yon bon pwochen etap.

Many families end up using more than one type at different stages of life — term while raising children and paying off a mortgage, transitioning toward whole life or IBC as income grows and legacy planning becomes more of a priority.

Anpil fanmi fini lè yo itilize plis pase yon kalite nan diferan etap nan lavi — tèm pandan y ap leve timoun ak peye yon ipotèk, tranzisyon vè lavi antye oswa IBC kòm revni grandi epi planifikasyon eritaj vin yon pi gwo priyorite.
