How to Form an LLC in Florida: A Step-by-Step Guide

Kijan pou Fòme yon LLC ann Florid: Yon Gid Etap pa Etap

Miguelson Etienne
Founder, ME Shield Financial Services
Fondatè, ME Shield Financial Services

Every year, thousands of Floridians start a small business out of a spare room, a truck, or a laptop at the kitchen table — and many of them operate for months or years without ever forming a proper legal entity. That gap can be costly: without an LLC, there's no real separation between your business debts and your personal assets.

Chak ane, dè milye moun Florid kòmanse yon ti biznis nan yon chanm vid, yon kamyon, oswa yon laptop sou tab kuizin. Anpil ladan yo fonksyone pandan mwa oswa ane san yo pa janm fòme yon antite legal pwòp. Vid sa a ka koute chè: san yon LLC, pa gen vrè separasyon ant dèt biznis ou ak byen pèsonèl ou.

Forming an LLC (Limited Liability Company) in Florida is more straightforward than most people expect. Here's exactly what the process involves, step by step.

Fòme yon LLC (Sosyete Reskonsabilite Limite) ann Florid pi senp pase sa pifò moun panse. Men egzakteman sa pwosesis la enplike, etap pa etap.

Step 1: Choose and Check Your Business Name

Etap 1: Chwazi ak Verifye Non Biznis Ou

Your LLC name must be distinguishable from other businesses already registered in Florida, and must include "LLC" or "Limited Liability Company" somewhere in the name. You can check name availability for free through the Florida Division of Corporations' online database before filing anything.

Non LLC ou dwe diferan de lòt biznis ki deja anrejistre ann Florid, epi li dwe gen "LLC" oswa "Limited Liability Company" yon kote nan non an. Ou ka verifye disponiblite non an gratis atravè baz done anliy Divizyon Konpayi Florid anvan ou depoze anyen.

Step 2: Designate a Registered Agent

Etap 2: Designe yon Ajan Anrejistre

Every Florida LLC must have a registered agent — a person or company with a physical Florida address who agrees to accept legal documents on the business's behalf. This can be you, a partner, or a registered agent service.

Chak LLC Florid dwe gen yon ajan anrejistre — yon moun oswa konpayi ki gen yon adrès fizik Florid ki dakò pou aksepte dokiman legal pou biznis la. Sa a ka ou menm, yon patnè, oswa yon sèvis ajan anrejistre.

Step 3: File Articles of Organization

Etap 3: Depoze Atik Òganizasyon

This is the actual document that legally creates your LLC. It's filed with the Florida Division of Corporations (commonly known by its filing portal, Sunbiz) and includes your business name, principal address, registered agent information, and the names of those managing the LLC.

Sa a se dokiman aktyèl ki kreye LLC ou legalman. Li depoze ak Divizyon Konpayi Florid (yo konnen kòmsadi pòtay depo li a, Sunbiz) epi li gen non biznis ou, adrès prensipal, enfòmasyon ajan anrejistre, ak non moun k ap jere LLC a.

Step 4: Get an EIN From the IRS

Etap 4: Jwenn yon EIN Nan IRS

An EIN (Employer Identification Number) is essentially a Social Security Number for your business. You'll need it to open a business bank account, hire employees, and file business taxes. Applying directly through the IRS is free.

Yon EIN (Nimewo Idantifikasyon Anplwayè) se esansyèlman yon Nimewo Sekirite Sosyal pou biznis ou. Ou pral bezwen li pou louvri yon kont bank biznis, anplwaye anplwaye, ak depoze taks biznis. Aplike dirèkteman atravè IRS gratis.

Step 5: Create an Operating Agreement

Etap 5: Kreye yon Akò Operasyon

Florida doesn't legally require an operating agreement, but skipping one is a common mistake. This internal document spells out ownership percentages, how profits are split, and what happens if a member wants to leave — details that prevent serious disputes later, especially in multi-member LLCs.

Florid pa egzije legalman yon akò operasyon, men sote youn se yon erè komen. Dokiman entèn sa a presize pousantaj posesyon, kijan pwofi separe, ak sa k ap pase si yon manm vle kite — detay ki anpeche konfli serye pita, espesyalman nan LLC ki gen plizyè manm.

Step 6: Open a Business Bank Account

Etap 6: Louvri yon Kont Bank Biznis

Once your EIN is issued, separate your business and personal money immediately. Mixing the two — known as "commingling" — is one of the fastest ways to lose the liability protection an LLC is supposed to provide.

Yon fwa EIN ou bay, separe lajan biznis ak pèsonèl ou imedyatman. Mele de a — yo rele "commingling" — se youn nan fason ki pi rapid pou pèdi pwoteksyon reskonsabilite yon LLC sipoze bay.

Step 7: File Your Annual Report — Every Year

Etap 7: Depoze Rapò Anyèl Ou — Chak Ane

Florida requires every LLC to file an Annual Report between January 1 and May 1 each year to remain in active status. Miss it, and the state can administratively dissolve your LLC — a surprisingly common and avoidable mistake.

Florid egzije chak LLC depoze yon Rapò Anyèl ant 1ye Janvye ak 1ye Me chak ane pou rete nan estati aktif. Rate li, epi eta a ka dissoud LLC ou administrativman — yon erè ki sezisman komen epi evitab.
