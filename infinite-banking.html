What Is Infinite Banking (IBC), in Plain Terms?

Kisa Infinite Banking (IBC) Ye, an Tèm Senp?

"Instead of paying interest to a bank every time you need money, Infinite Banking lets you become the banker — and pay that interest back to yourself." — ME Shield Financial Services

"Olye peye enterè bank chak fwa ou bezwen lajan, Infinite Banking kite ou vin bankye a — epi peye enterè sa a tounen pou tèt ou." — ME Shield Financial Services

The Infinite Banking Concept uses a specially designed whole life insurance policy as your own private banking system. Instead of keeping savings in a traditional bank account earning very little interest, you build cash value inside the policy — and then you can borrow against that cash value for major purchases, business investments, or emergencies, all while your full policy value keeps growing. Done correctly, you become your own source of financing instead of paying interest to a bank every time you need capital.

Konsèp Infinite Banking la itilize yon kontra asirans whole life ki fèt espesyalman kòm pwòp sistèm bankè prive ou. Olye kenbe ekonomi nan yon kont bank tradisyonèl ki fè trè piti enterè, ou bati valè kach anndan kontra a — epi apre sa ou ka prete kont valè kach sa a pou gwo acha, envestisman biznis, oswa ijans, pandan tout valè kontra ou kontinye grandi. Fè kòrèkteman, ou vin pwòp sous finansman ou olye peye enterè bank chak fwa ou bezwen kapital.

This is not a fit for everyone, and it isn't a replacement for term life insurance in every situation — it's a long-term strategy that works best for people who are disciplined about contributions and thinking years ahead, not a quick investment play. As an IBC Practitioner, I walk you through how the strategy actually works, whether it fits your financial goals, and how it compares to more familiar options like whole life or universal life insurance — explained clearly, with no pressure.

Sa a se pa yon bagay ki fèt pou tout moun, epi li pa ranplase asirans term life nan tout sitiyasyon — se yon estrateji alontèm ki mache pi byen pou moun ki disipline nan kontribisyon yo epi k ap panse plizyè ane devan. Kòm yon Praktisyen IBC, mwen gide ou nan fason estrateji a reyèlman fonksyone, si li koresponn ak objektif finansyè ou, ak kijan li konpare ak opsyon pi familye tankou whole life oswa universal life — eksplike klèman, san presyon.
