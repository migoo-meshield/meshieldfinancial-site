The 2026 Standard Deduction Just Went Up — Here's What It Means for Your Refund

Dediksyon Estanda 2026 Fèk Monte — Men Sa Sa Vle Di Pou Ranbousman Ou
← Back to Blog ← Retounen nan Blòg
Tax TipsKonsèy Taks·July 2026

Every year, the IRS adjusts the standard deduction to keep up with inflation, and 2026 brought another increase. If you're not itemizing your deductions, the standard deduction is the amount the IRS lets you subtract from your income before calculating what you owe — so a higher number generally means less taxable income.

The New 2026 Numbers

Filing Status2026 Standard Deduction
Single / Married Filing Separately$16,100
Married Filing Jointly$32,200
Head of Household$24,150

These figures apply to tax returns you'll file in 2027 for the 2026 tax year — so you won't see the effect on a return until then, but it can affect how much is withheld from your paycheck starting now.

What Changed Beyond the Standard Deduction

A few other 2026 figures worth knowing: the Earned Income Tax Credit maximum rose to $8,231 for taxpayers with three or more qualifying children, the annual gift tax exclusion stays at $19,000, and the additional deduction for taxpayers 65 and older (from the 2025 tax law) continues to apply, phasing out above certain income levels.

What This Means for You

A higher standard deduction generally means slightly less of your income is taxed — which can mean a small bump in your take-home pay or a slightly larger refund next year, depending on your withholding. It won't change much for everyone, but if you've had a major life change (new job, new baby, marriage), this is a good year to double-check your withholding.

General education, not personalized advice. Every tax situation is different. These figures come from official IRS guidance current as of publication, but your actual liability depends on your full financial picture. Talk to a tax professional before making decisions based on this article.

Should You Do Anything Right Now?

Not urgently — but if it's been a while since you checked your W-4 withholding, mid-year is a smart time to review it, especially if last year's refund (or tax bill) surprised you.

Ki Lòt Bagay Ki Chanje Pase Dediksyon Estanda A

Kèk lòt chif 2026 ki vo konnen: maksimòm Kredi Taks Revni Touche a monte a $8,231 pou kontribyab ki gen twa oswa plis timoun ki kalifye, esklizyon taks kado anyèl la rete nan $19,000, epi dediksyon adisyonèl pou kontribyab 65 ane oswa plis (soti nan lwa taks 2025 la) kontinye aplike, ki disparèt pwogresivman anwo sèten nivo revni.

Sa Sa Vle Di Pou Ou

Yon dediksyon estanda pi wo jeneralman vle di yon ti kras mwens nan revni ou ki taksab — sa ka vle di yon ti ogmantasyon nan chèk ou oswa yon ranbousman yon ti kras pi gwo ane pwochèn, depann sou retansyon ou. Li pap chanje anpil pou tout moun, men si ou te gen yon gwo chanjman nan lavi (nouvo travay, nouvo bebe, maryaj), se yon bon ane pou verifye retansyon ou de fwa.

Edikasyon jeneral, se pa konsèy pèsonalize. Chak sitiyasyon taks diferan. Chif sa yo soti nan direktiv ofisyèl IRS aktyèl nan moman piblikasyon an, men obligasyon reyèl ou depann sou tout sitiyasyon finansyè ou. Pale ak yon pwofesyonèl taks anvan ou pran desizyon baze sou atik sa a.
