Infinite Banking, Explained Simply

Bankin Enfini, Eksplike Senpleman
Miguelson Etienne
Founder, ME Shield Financial Services
Fondatè, ME Shield Financial Services

Every time I bring up Infinite Banking with a new client, I get the same reaction: raised eyebrows and "wait, how does that actually work?" So let's slow down and go through it in plain language, without the jargon.

Chak fwa mwen pale de Bankin Enfini ak yon nouvo kliyan, mwen jwenn menm reyaksyon an: sousi leve ak "tann, kijan sa reyèlman fonksyone?" Ann ralanti epi ale nan li nan lang senp, san jagon.

Educational content only — this is not financial advice, and IBC isn't the right fit for everyone. Kontni edikatif sèlman — sa a se pa konsèy finansye, epi IBC pa apwopriye pou tout moun.

The Core Idea

Lide Santral la

Infinite Banking Concept (IBC) is a strategy built around using a specially designed whole life insurance policy as your own personal banking system. Instead of borrowing from a traditional bank and paying them interest, you build cash value inside your policy, then borrow against that cash value when you need funds — paying interest back to your own policy instead of an outside lender.

Konsèp Bankin Enfini (IBC) se yon estrateji ki bati alantou itilize yon kontra asirans vi antye espesyalman fèt kòm pwòp sistèm bankin pèsonèl ou. Olye prete nan yon bank tradisyonèl epi peye yo enterè, ou bati valè lajan anndan kontra ou, epi apre prete kont valè lajan sa a lè ou bezwen fon — peye enterè tounen nan pwòp kontra ou olye yon prèt deyò.

You're not avoiding debt. You're changing who benefits from the interest you're already paying throughout your life.

Ou pa evite dèt. Ou ap chanje ki moun ki benefisye nan enterè ou deja peye pandan tout lavi ou.

Why Whole Life Insurance (Not Term)

Poukisa Asirans Vi Antye (Pa Term)

Term life insurance is pure protection — it doesn't build cash value, and it expires. Whole life insurance is different: a portion of your premium builds cash value over time, and that cash value grows in a way that's generally protected from market volatility. That stability is what makes it usable as a banking tool.

Asirans vi term se pwoteksyon pi — li pa bati valè lajan, epi li ekspire. Asirans vi antye diferan: yon pòsyon prim ou bati valè lajan avèk tan, epi valè lajan sa a grandi nan yon fason ki jeneralman pwoteje kont vòlatilite mache a. Estabilite sa a se sa ki fè li itilizab kòm yon zouti bankin.

How the "Banking" Part Actually Works

Kijan Pati "Bankin" an Reyèlman Fonksyone

  1. You pay premiums into a properly structured whole life policy.
  2. Ou peye prim nan yon kontra vi antye byen estriktire.
  3. Cash value builds inside the policy over time.
  4. Valè lajan bati anndan kontra a avèk tan.
  5. When you need funds, you take a policy loan against that cash value instead of going to a bank.
  6. Lè ou bezwen fon, ou pran yon prè kontra kont valè lajan sa a olye ale nan yon bank.
  7. You repay the loan on your own terms, with interest that effectively goes back into your own financial system.
  8. Ou ranbouse prè a nan pwòp tèm ou, ak enterè ki efektivman tounen nan pwòp sistèm finansye ou.
  9. Meanwhile, your full cash value typically continues earning inside the policy, even while a portion is loaned out.
  10. Antretan, valè lajan konplè ou tipikman kontinye touche anndan kontra a, menm pandan yon pòsyon prete deyò.

What IBC Is Not

Sa IBC Pa Ye

I want to be clear about this, because there's a lot of misinformation out there. IBC is not a way to get rich quick. It's not a replacement for having no debt at all. It's not guaranteed to outperform every other financial strategy. It's a long-term discipline — the policies are usually structured over years, and the real benefit compounds over time, not overnight.

Mwen vle klè sou sa a, paske gen anpil move enfòmasyon deyò a. IBC se pa yon fason pou vin rich vit. Li se pa yon ranplasman pou pa gen okenn dèt ditou. Li pa garanti pou depase tout lòt estrateji finansye. Se yon disiplin alontèm — kontra yo tipikman estriktire sou plizyè ane, epi vrè benefis la konpoze avèk tan, se pa yon jou pou lòt.

Who Tends to Benefit From This Strategy

Ki Moun ki Tipikman Benefisye de Estrateji Sa a

IBC tends to make the most sense for people who already have consistent income, who are disciplined about consistently funding a policy, and who want more control over their own capital instead of routing every major purchase through a traditional bank. It's not for someone looking for a quick return or who can't commit to consistent premium payments.

IBC tipikman fè plis sans pou moun ki deja gen revni konsistan, ki disipline sou finanse yon kontra konsistaman, epi ki vle plis kontwòl sou pwòp kapital yo olye wout chak gwo acha atravè yon bank tradisyonèl. Li se pa pou yon moun k ap chèche yon retou rapid oswa ki pa ka angaje pou peman prim konsistan.

Èske Sa a Bon pou Ou?
