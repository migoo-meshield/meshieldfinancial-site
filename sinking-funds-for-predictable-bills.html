Predictable Bills Are Not Emergencies: Build a Simple Sinking Fund

Fakti Ou Prevwa Pa Ijans: Prepare Yon Fon pou Yo

A practical way to set money aside for annual bills while keeping emergency savings separate.

Yon fason pratik pou mete lajan sou kote pou fakti anyèl san melanje li ak epay ijans.

Give planned bills their own line

Bay fakti ou prevwa yo pwòp liy pa yo

A car registration, annual insurance premium or planned school expense can surprise your checking account even when it is predictable. A sinking fund is simply money reserved gradually for a known future expense. It does not require a special financial product. Keep the purpose clear so a scheduled bill does not repeatedly drain the money you intended for a true emergency.

Enskripsyon machin, prim asirans anyèl oswa depans lekòl planifye ka siprann kont ou menm lè ou konnen yo ap vini. Yon sinking fund se senpleman lajan ou mete piti piti pou yon depans ou konnen. Ou pa bezwen yon pwodui finansye espesyal pou sa. Kenbe objektif la klè pou yon fakti planifye pa toujou vide lajan ou te mete pou yon vrè ijans.

Use the amount and the time remaining

Itilize montan an ak tan ki rete a

Example: an illustrative $1,200 bill due in 12 months needs $100 per month. If it is due in six months, the target becomes $200 per month. Subtract anything already saved before dividing. These examples ignore account interest and price changes. Write the due date beside the target so you can adjust when a renewal notice arrives.

Egzanp: yon fakti ipotetik $1,200 ki vini nan 12 mwa bezwen $100 pa mwa. Si li vini nan sis mwa, objektif la vin $200 pa mwa. Retire sa ou gentan sere anvan ou divize. Egzanp sa yo pa konte enterè kont ni chanjman pri. Ekri dat limit la bò kote objektif la pou ou ajiste lè avi renouvèlman an rive.

Start with two or three categories

Kòmanse ak de oswa twa kategori

Choose the next few bills that cause the most disruption: insurance, car maintenance or school costs, for example. Use labeled savings buckets or a simple spreadsheet with a running balance. Keep near-term bill money accessible and review the account’s fees and protections. You do not need a separate paid app or a new account for every category.

Chwazi kèk fakti ki deranje bidjè a plis: asirans, antretyen machin oswa lekòl, pa egzanp. Itilize ti seksyon epay ki gen non oswa yon tablo ak balans k ap mete ajou. Kenbe lajan fakti ki toupre yo aksesib epi verifye frè ak pwoteksyon kont la. Ou pa bezwen yon aplikasyon peye ni yon nouvo kont pou chak kategori.

When the target does not fit

Lè objektif la pa antre nan bidjè a

Suppose the calculation calls for $200 but you can spare $80. Record the $120 monthly gap now, while there is time to change the plan. Consider reducing the planned expense, moving a discretionary purchase or discussing available payment terms. A smaller realistic contribution is more useful than a perfect target you immediately abandon.

Sipoze kalkil la mande $200 men ou ka mete $80. Ekri diferans $120 chak mwa a kounye a pandan gen tan pou chanje plan an. Konsidere diminye depans planifye a, ranvwaye yon acha ki pa esansyèl oswa pale sou kondisyon peman. Yon ti kontribisyon ou ka kenbe pi itil pase yon objektif pafè ou abandone touswit.

Review after every payment

Revize apre chak peman

After paying the bill, save the receipt, reset the target and check the next due date. Keep a separate emergency goal for unexpected setbacks; the CFPB’s emergency-fund guidance explains that purpose. This article’s budgeting worksheet is an original organizational example, not investment advice or a promise that a particular savings target fits everyone.

Apre ou peye fakti a, sove resi a, rekòmanse objektif la epi verifye pwochen dat la. Kenbe yon objektif ijans apa pou kou di ou pa prevwa; gid CFPB sou fon ijans esplike objektif sa a. Tablo bidjè nan atik sa a se yon egzanp òganizasyon orijinal, se pa konsèy envestisman ni pwomès yon sèl objektif adapte pou tout moun.
