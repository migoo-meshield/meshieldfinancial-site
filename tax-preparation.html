Individual, Self-Employed, and Business Tax Returns — Done Right

Deklarasyon Taks Endividyèl, Travayè Endepandan, ak Biznis — Fè Byen

Filing your own taxes with software can work fine for a simple W-2 return. But once you're self-employed, running a small business, claiming dependents, or dealing with multiple income sources, mistakes get expensive — missed deductions cost you money, and errors can trigger IRS notices or delayed refunds.

Ranpli pwòp taks ou ak lojisyèl ka mache byen pou yon senp deklarasyon W-2. Men yon fwa ou travayè endepandan, w ap dirije yon ti biznis, ou reklame moun ki depann sou ou, oswa ou gen plizyè sous revni, erè yo vin chè — dediksyon manke koute ou lajan, epi erè yo ka deklanche avi IRS oswa ranbousman ki retade.

As an IRS PTIN-registered Tax Specialist, I prepare individual, self-employed, and small business returns with a focus on accuracy and every deduction you actually qualify for. That means reviewing your specific situation instead of running you through a generic checklist — self-employed clients get help finding write-offs they'd otherwise miss, and families get help claiming every credit available to them. Every return is explained clearly before it's filed, in English or Kreyòl, so you understand exactly what's being submitted on your behalf.

Kòm yon Espesyalis Taks anrejistre nan IRS PTIN, mwen prepare deklarasyon endividyèl, travayè endepandan, ak ti biznis avèk yon konsantrasyon sou presizyon ak chak dediksyon ou reyèlman kalifye pou li. Sa vle di egzaminen sitiyasyon espesifik ou olye pase ou nan yon lis jeneral — kliyan travayè endepandan yo jwenn èd pou jwenn dediksyon yo ta manke otreman, epi fanmi yo jwenn èd pou reklame chak kredi ki disponib pou yo. Chak deklarasyon eksplike klèman anvan li depoze, an Anglè oswa Kreyòl, pou ou konprann egzakteman sa k ap soumèt nan non ou.
