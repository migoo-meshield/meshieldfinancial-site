ABOUT ME SHIELD FINANCIAL SERVICES SOU ME SHIELD FINANCIAL SERVICES

ME Shield Financial Services is a Florida-based practice offering insurance, tax preparation, business filing, and immigration forms filing — all in one place. Whether you're insuring your family, filing your taxes, handling immigration paperwork, or registering a new business, you get the same consultant, the same level of care, and answers in plain English or Kreyòl Ayisyen.

ME Shield Financial Services se yon pratik ki baze ann Florid ki ofri asirans, preparasyon taks, depo biznis, ak ranpli fòmilè imigrasyon — tout nan yon sèl kote. Kit ou ap asire fanmi ou, depoze taks ou, jere papye imigrasyon, oswa enskri yon nouvo biznis, ou jwenn menm konsiltan an, menm nivo swen an, ak repons nan Anglè senp oswa Kreyòl Ayisyen.

OUR STORY
ISTWA NOU

Why ME Shield Exists

Poukisa ME Shield Egziste

ME Shield Group LLC started with a simple observation: too many families in Florida's Haitian Creole-speaking community were navigating insurance enrollment, tax season, and immigration paperwork without anyone who could walk them through it in their own language — or with the kind of personal attention a major decision deserves.

ME Shield Group LLC kòmanse ak yon obsèvasyon senp: twòp fanmi nan kominote Kreyòl Ayisyen ann Florid t ap navige enskripsyon asirans, sezon taks, ak papye imigrasyon san pèsonn ki ka pase atravè yo nan pwòp lang yo — oswa ak kalite atansyon pèsonèl yon gwo desizyon merite.

ME Shield Group LLC, operating as ME Shield Financial Services, was built to close that gap directly: one licensed consultant, five connected services — insurance, tax preparation, business filing, immigration forms filing, and the Infinite Banking Concept — with a commitment to explain everything clearly, in English or Kreyòl Ayisyen, with no jargon and no rush.

ME Shield Group LLC, k ap fonksyone kòm ME Shield Financial Services, te bati pou femen vid sa a dirèkteman: yon sèl konsiltan lisansye, senk sèvis ki konekte — asirans, preparasyon taks, depo biznis, ranpli fòmilè imigrasyon, ak Konsèp Bank Enfini — ak yon angajman pou eksplike tout bagay klèman, an Anglè oswa Kreyòl Ayisyen, san jagon ak san kouri.

The name reflects the goal plainly: a shield is protection, and that's what every conversation here is built around — protecting your family's finances, your tax refund, and your immigration paperwork with the same care you'd want for your own.

Non an reflekte objektif la klèman: yon "shield" se pwoteksyon, epi se sa chak konvèsasyon isit la bati sou li — pwoteje finans fanmi ou, ranbousman taks ou, ak papye imigrasyon ou ak menm swen ou ta vle pou pwòp tèt ou.
