Becoming a U.S. Citizen: Understanding the Naturalization Process

Vin Yon Sitwayen Ameriken: Konprann Pwosesis Natiralizasyon An

Miguelson Etienne
Founder, ME Shield Financial Services
Fondatè, ME Shield Financial Services

After years of holding a Green Card, many permanent residents put off the next step — becoming a U.S. citizen — simply because the process feels unfamiliar. The path is more structured than it might seem, with clear eligibility requirements and a predictable sequence of steps.

Apre ane ki kenbe yon Green Card, anpil rezidan pèmanan mete sou kote pwochen etap la — vin yon sitwayen Ameriken — senpleman paske pwosesis la santi familye. Chemen an pi estriktire pase li ka parèt, ak egzijans kalifikasyon klè ak yon sekans etap previzib.

Basic Eligibility Requirements

Egzijans Kalifikasyon Debaz

  • Generally, you must have held a Green Card for at least 5 years (or 3 years if married to and living with a U.S. citizen).
  • Jeneralman, ou dwe te kenbe yon Green Card pou omwen 5 ane (oswa 3 ane si marye ak epi ap viv ak yon sitwayen Ameriken).
  • Continuous residence in the U.S. for that period, with no single absence longer than one year.
  • Rezidans kontinyèl nan Etazini pou peryòd sa a, san okenn absans pi long pase yon ane.
  • Physical presence in the U.S. for at least half of the required residence period.
  • Prezans fizik nan Etazini pou omwen mwatye nan peryòd rezidans obligatwa a.
  • Good moral character, and basic English and U.S. civics knowledge (with some exceptions based on age or disability).
  • Bon karaktè moral, ak konesans debaz Anglè ak sivik Ameriken (ak kèk eksepsyon baze sou laj oswa andikap).

The Application: Form N-400

Aplikasyon an: Fòm N-400

Form N-400 is the official Application for Naturalization. It asks for your residence history, employment, family information, and a series of eligibility questions. Accuracy matters here — inconsistencies between your N-400 and your prior immigration paperwork can cause delays or requests for additional evidence.

Fòm N-400 se Aplikasyon ofisyèl pou Natiralizasyon. Li mande istwa rezidans ou, anplwa, enfòmasyon fanmi, ak yon seri kesyon kalifikasyon. Presizyon enpòtan isit la — enkonsistans ant N-400 ou ak papye imigrasyon anvan ou ka lakòz reta oswa demann pou prèv adisyonèl.

The Biometrics Appointment

Randevou Byometrik

After filing, USCIS schedules a biometrics appointment to collect fingerprints, a photo, and a signature, used for background and security checks.

Apre depo, USCIS pwograme yon randevou byometrik pou kolekte anprent dwèt, yon foto, ak yon siyati, itilize pou verifikasyon background ak sekirite.

The Interview and Civics Test

Entèvyou ak Egzamen Sivik

This is the step most applicants feel nervous about, but it's designed to be straightforward if you've prepared. A USCIS officer reviews your application with you, tests your English reading, writing, and speaking ability, and asks you civics questions from an official study list of up to 100 possible questions — you'll only need to answer a handful correctly.

Sa a se etap pifò aplikan santi yo nève sou li, men li fèt pou senp si ou te prepare. Yon ofisye USCIS revize aplikasyon ou avèk ou, teste kapasite lekti, ekri, ak pale Anglè ou, epi mande ou kesyon sivik nan yon lis etid ofisyèl jiska 100 kesyon posib — ou pral sèlman bezwen reponn yon ti ponyen kòrèkteman.

The Oath of Allegiance

Sèman Alejyans

If approved, the final step is a ceremony where you take the Oath of Allegiance — the moment you officially become a U.S. citizen. From that point, you're eligible for a U.S. passport, the right to vote, and other rights and protections that don't extend to permanent residents.

Si apwouve, dènye etap la se yon seremoni kote ou pran Sèman Alejyans la — moman ou ofisyèlman vin yon sitwayen Ameriken. Apati pwen sa a, ou kalifye pou yon paspò Ameriken, dwa pou vote, ak lòt dwa ak pwoteksyon ki pa pwolonje a rezidan pèmanan.
